Behind The Shell

The Mastery of Bonbon Artistry

At CACAOIRE, we treat bonbons as edible canvases. Our process begins with pure Valrhona dark chocolate shells temper-tuned to deliver a crisp, resonant snap upon bite.

Inside, each shell holds cooked caramel made from French cultured butter, ganache infused with fresh botanical herbs, or nutty pralines slow-roasted to golden perfection.
